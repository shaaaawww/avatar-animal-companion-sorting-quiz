Part II / Field index

The creature bestiary

Part III / The animal guides

Named companions and spirits

Species and individuals are different: Appa is one flying bison, while Naga is one polar bear dog. These characters have their own stories and are not counted as separate species in the index.

Sources and scope

The 2025 Beasts of the Four Nations book focuses on documented creatures in the Aang and Korra eras. The broader list here also contains creatures from franchise lore beyond those entries, plus newly introduced cat-monkeys. Some names originate in dialogue or ancillary material; inclusions are not equally prominent or equally suited to being kept as pets. Dangerous wildlife and spirits are fantasy matches only.
